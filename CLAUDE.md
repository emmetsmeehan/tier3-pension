# My Fire Pension: FDNY Tier 2 and Tier 3 pension calculators

A public, single-page guide and pension calculator for FDNY uniformed members in Tier 3 (appointed on or after July 1, 2009). The owner is the GitHub user `emmetsmeehan`. He built it to share with fellow members, who will use it when thinking about real retirement decisions, so the numbers and the wording have to be right.

This repository is the source of truth. The calculator used to live on a claude.ai page; that copy is retired.

## How to work on it

- Make small, targeted edits. Do not rewrite the page to make a change. (The owner asked for one full redesign in Oct. 2026; that is done.)
- Most users are on phones and are not tech-savvy. Keep tap targets large, wording plain, and the answer close to the inputs. After a layout change, check about 390px and 320px wide, light and dark. The tight spots are the service slider's stop buttons (22 and 22½ sit side by side on the lower row), the earnings table and the comparison table.
- Every color is a CSS token defined for light and dark themes at the top of the stylesheet. Use the tokens; do not hard-code colors.
- After changing the script, confirm it parses, and test the affected formula at its boundaries (for example 24 years 11 months, 25, 29, 30 and 35 years for the longevity bonus).
- Check pay figures and legal claims against primary sources before putting them on the page. If something could not be verified, tell the owner plainly.
- When a rule changes, update every place that describes it: the hints in both calculators, the Pension basics answers, the fine print at the bottom of Pension basics, and the bill cards.
- The owner wants direct answers. When a request conflicts with a rule below, or the right reading is unclear, ask him before changing the math.

## Look and wording

Slightly rounded corners and simple line icons are approved (Oct. 2026). FDNY red is the accent. Do not add any mention of AI or Claude to the page.

## Page structure

Home (headline "Learn about your pension.", sub "Everything you need to know, and what you'll collect in retirement.") with four big buttons, then the question box. Each section opens as its own screen at a hash address with a sticky "Home" back button; the browser back button works:

- `#basics` Pension basics: four key-number tiles, tap-to-open questions in plain English (this replaced the old FAQ), and "The fine print: how the calculators work".
- `#bills` Bills in Albany: cards built from the `BILLS` array in the script. Each card: title, introduced date, summary, a vertical status track (green checks for done steps, an amber pulsing clock for the step in progress, grey dashed circles for later steps; a law gets a gold pen on "Signed into law"), "Read the full bill" links at the bottom, and "See what it means for you" which turns that bill on in the personal calculator.
- `#quick` Quick calculator: final average salary, years (slider), 3/4s checkbox, and "If these bills pass" checkboxes. Shows only the first-year monthly pension, with a note on whether it grows (none, partial, full escalation, at 3% inflation). A typed FAS is used exactly as typed, with no growth.
- `#personal` Your personal numbers (renamed from "Your personal calculator" at the owner's request, Oct. 11, 2026; in running text it's "the personal numbers page"): 1 About you (dates), 2 Your earnings (choose "Type in each year" or "Import from NYCAPS"; one or the other, each keeps its own numbers), 3 Your retirement (3/4s, slider, bill checkboxes, Social Security, inflation under "More options"). Results: the headline card (sticky on desktop) leads with the first-year total, pension plus VSF, a year and a month; then a breakdown (pension with the longevity bonus called out as included, VSF, total), the banked variable as a highlighted lump sum, and "Your pension grows X% a year", which opens a compact year-by-year table (pension a month, pension plus VSF a year, the Social Security offset at 62 marked). Then "Set a goal", comparison of 20, 23 and 25 years, and the power of escalation. On phones a bar at the bottom shows the monthly pension and jumps to the results.
- Personal numbers page details (Oct. 12, 2026, owner's decisions): the dates in step 1 start blank (they used to be prefilled with example dates, `EX_DOB`/`EX_APPT`, which saved states still holding both are cleared on load; "try it with example numbers" fills them in if blank). An appointment date on the wrong side of July 1, 2009 shows "You're probably in Tier 2" (or 3 on the Tier 2 page) under the date, with a "Switch to Tier 2/3" button; it carries the two dates over in the address (`?dob=…&appt=…`), and the other page uses them only where its own dates are blank, then cleans the address. Until results show, a "What you'll see" card sits under the page intro. Ages read "50 and 11 months" (tables: "50 yrs 11 mo"), not decimals; on phones up to 400px the line under the service slider keeps room for two lines so the slider doesn't jump. The comparison table shows date, age, monthly first year, banked variable, yearly at 62 and total through 85 (in millions, "$2.49M", on phones); "Show all rows" opens the rest. Tier 2 does the same with its own rows.

## Calculation rules

These are the rules the page implements. They are the owner's decisions; do not change them without asking.

- **Pension:** 50% of final average salary (FAS) at 20 or more years of service, under S4727 (Chapter 692 of the Laws of 2025, signed Dec. 19, 2025). Tier 3 is capped at 50%.
- **FAS:** the best 3 consecutive calendar years. (The Fund's Tier 3 Enhanced SPD, Dec. 2019 revision in the owner's Drive, says 3 years only for members who joined July 1, 2009 to March 31, 2012, and 5 years, each capped at 110% of the prior 4 years' average, for "modified Tier 3" members from April 1, 2012. Shown to the owner Oct. 12, 2026; he chose to keep 3 years for everyone.) A year counts only up to 110% of the average of the two years before it. The owner confirmed 3 years (Oct. 2026), though the April 2024 plan description says 5. The personal calculator uses full calendar years before the retirement year.
- **10% limit display:** the earnings table flags "over the 10% limit" only on best-3 years and on years from the 5th year on; rookie raises often top it but those years never count (owner, Oct. 12, 2026). The math is unchanged.
- **Future years:** by default each year after the last known one repeats that year's pay. A checkbox fills them with a yearly raise (default 2.5%) instead. Future years after this one are folded under "Show future years".
- **Escalation:** the lesser of CPI or 3%, compounding, on the whole pension. (CPI above 3% is banked and applied in later years, per the Part ZZ fiscal note; the math doesn't model it, since inflation is assumed constant. The owner had it explained in the Pension basics escalation answer, Oct. 12, 2026.) Full escalation needs collection to start at the **23-year** mark (FY2027 state budget, signed May 27, 2026; it was 25). The rate drops by 1/36 for each month collection starts earlier, so 20 years gets none. `LAW_ESC` in the script.
- **Longevity bonus (law):** (also applied on the Tier 2 page since Oct. 12, 2026; the enacted law covers all plans) at 25+ years, 5% of the rank's top pay plus 1% for each year past 25, capped at 15% at 35, added straight to FAS (FY2027 state budget, signed May 27, 2026; originally S9202/A10392). Always applied for service retirements, never for 3/4s. `LAW_BONUS` in the script. The rank picker shows in the personal calculator, and in the quick calculator at 25+ years. The page assumes collection starts at retirement. It used to offer deferring collection to the 25-year date; the owner had it removed (Oct. 2026) because the option was confusing.
- **COLA:** without full escalation, the pension gets the greater of escalation or COLA. COLA is half of CPI (minimum 1%, maximum 3%) on the first $18,000 only, starting at age 62 after 5 years retired or age 55 after 10 years retired. COLA reduces the VSF until 62.
- **VSF and banked variable:** the Variable Supplements Fund pays $12,000 a year to service retirees with 20 or more years. Each year worked past 20 banks one payment, paid as a lump sum at retirement. The $12,000 is fixed in the code; the owner had the editable assumption removed.
- **Social Security offset:** at 62 the pension drops by 50% of the primary Social Security benefit.
- **3/4s disability:** a large "3/4s (Disabled)" checkbox above the service slider. From the Fire Pension Fund's Tier 3 Enhanced Summary Plan Description (UFA copy dated April 18, 2024, PDF from the owner Oct. 4, 2026): accident disability pays 75% of FAS at any service, generally not taxed; no escalation (COLA only, after 5 years retired at any age); no VSF or banked variable; no Social Security offset; health insurance regardless of service; no workers' comp offset mentioned. The owner decided: the checkbox is in both calculators; no longevity bonus on disability; no 20/22½/25 comparison (the comparison, escalation section, bill checkboxes and Social Security field are hidden); slider runs from 1 year with stops every 5 years. Ordinary (non-line-of-duty) disability is not modeled. A typed FAS is not shrunk for service under 20 years.
- **Earnings import:** from the NYCAPS ESS "Tax Summary for All Years" table (Pay and Tax Information tile, then Pay and Tax Information > Tax Summary (W-2, 1127 & 1095C)), by copy and paste or by uploading a screenshot. It uses the Medicare Wages column (the third number after the year) and skips the current unfinished year. Pasted lines with a year and one number are also accepted. Screenshots are read on the device with Tesseract.js; because recognition drops commas and decimal points, screenshot amounts are read as digits with the last two as cents (every Tax Summary amount has cents). Either way the member checks the numbers on a review screen (amounts under $5,000 or over $400,000 are flagged), then locks them in. Locked years show "Verified" and can't be edited; other years can be typed. "Start over" clears the import. Once locked, the earnings table folds behind a "Your earnings, year by year" button (closed again on each visit), and if the dates in step 1 are blank a red "Add your dates to see your results" box with "Go to step 1" shows under the verified bar (owner, Oct. 12, 2026; both tiers). If ESS changes its menu names or columns, update the steps on the page and the parser. Tested with mock screenshots at computer, sideways-phone and upright-phone sizes, and a cut-off one; not yet with a real NYCAPS screenshot.
- **Goal:** the member enters a monthly pension goal. The page shows the FAS it takes (goal × 24, or × 16 on a 3/4s), the earliest retirement (by month, up to 35 years or age 62) where their earnings reach it, or, if they never do, the pay needed in the 3 years before their chosen retirement.
- **Service slider:** 20 to 35 years, with quick stops at 20, 23 (lower row), 25, 30 and 35. The comparison is 20, 23 and 25 years. The readout shows years on one line and months underneath ("0 months" on whole years), so its width doesn't change as the slider moves.

### Bills

**Became law May 27, 2026, in the FY2027 state budget: S9008-C / A10008-C (the TED Article VII bill), Parts ZZ and AAA.** Text read Oct. 12, 2026 from the enacted bill PDF the owner put in Drive (`A10008c.pdf`); chapter number not printed in the bill. Part ZZ adds RSSL § 510(b)(2-a): full escalation date at 23 years for uniformed FIRE members (fiscal note 2026-11: partial escalation now from 20 years; escalation above 3% a year is banked and applied in later years). Part AAA adds Admin Code § 15-110.1, word for word S9202, covering any member "under the plan in which they are enrolled" (Tier 2 too). Part FFF (corrections/sanitation) confirms Chapter 692 of 2025 set FIRE normal retirement age at 20 years. PPGG (A10005-C) has no pension Parts. Because they passed inside the budget, the standalone bill pages on nysenate.gov still say "in committee"; the bill cards explain that.
- Full escalation at 23 years: S9204 (Jackson) / A11269 (Pheffer Amato).
- Longevity bonus, +1% a year: S9202 / A10392. The competing steps version (S9306 / A10438: 5/10/15%) did not pass and was removed from the page.

**Pending:** S9203 / A10254, full escalation at 20 years. In Senate Civil Service and Pensions as of Oct. 4, 2026; Assembly status not confirmed. Checkbox, off by default.
**Pending (added Oct. 12, 2026, owner's request):** S5058A (2025–26, Jackson / Scarcella-Spanton; amended 2026; in Senate Civil Service and Pensions; PDF from the owner). For Tier 3 it adds 1/40th of "final salary" for each year (or fraction) past 20, on top of the 50% (for Tier 2 it changes the 1/60th to 1/40th). Checkbox in both calculators (`b40`, `kind:'flag'` in `BILLS`; S9203 is `kind:'esc'`), off by default, hidden on 3/4s like the other bill checkboxes. The page figures it on best-3 FAS without the longevity bonus, prorated by month; the fiscal note read "final salary" as the last 12 months.

Other bills from the owner's union rep (not pension-math, not on the page yet, statuses unconfirmed): widows' COLA S10636/A11555, WTC 25-to-35 extension S10085/A11213 (one source says signed Sept. 10, 2026 as Chapter 295), widows' property tax exemption S9502A/A10885.

Each pending bill card says it's a 2025–26 bill that dies if not passed by Dec. 31, 2026 (`SESSION_END` in both scripts). In January 2027, check whether each was reintroduced and update or remove its card.

When any status changes, update its entry in the `BILLS` array (`step`, dates, `law`, `signed`, `chapter`, `note`), the "last checked" date in Bills in Albany, the Pension basics answers and the fine print.

| LT | $140,212 | UFOA rate schedule effective July 31, 2025 |

## Tier 2 page (`tier2/index.html`)

Added Oct. 11, 2026 at the owner's request, for members appointed July 1, 1973 to June 30, 2009. Served at https://myfirepension.com/tier2/. It is a separate single file with the same look, CSS and earnings import as the Tier 3 page; a fix to shared code (import parser, slider, question box) usually needs making in both files. It saves to localStorage under `fdny-tier2-v1`, separate from Tier 3. The Tier 3 home disclaimer, its "Am I in Tier 3?" answer and its Tier 2 warning link to it; the Tier 2 home links back only through the "Want to see Tier 3 instead?" button at the top (the owner had a separate Tier 3 card removed as confusing, Oct. 11, 2026). Same disclaimer rules. **Bills in Albany (added Oct. 12, 2026 at the owner's request, same design as Tier 3; `BILLS` array in the Tier 2 script):** pending S5058A (2025–26, Jackson / Scarcella-Spanton; amended 2026, fiscal note 2026-70 dated April 24, 2026; in Senate Civil Service and Pensions): Tier 2's 1/60th becomes 1/40th for service and 3/4s pensions (it also gives Tier 3 an extra 1/40th of final salary per year past 20; on the Tier 3 page too). It's the "If this bill passes" checkbox in both Tier 2 calculators (`b40` in saved state), off by default. Law card: Chapter 540 of 2021 (S6405). Not shown: S9593 (2024, same 1/40th change, died) and S5571A (2023–24, would make Chapter 540 retroactive for those already retired; died). Bill PDFs came from the owner; statuses are from the bill texts, not checked live (nysenate.gov is blocked from the session). Home now has four cards again. Question box subject: "Tier 2 calculator: new question".

Sources: the full Tiers 1 & 2 Summary Plan Description (Sept. 12, 2014; `pension_manual_tier_1_2.pdf` in the owner's Drive FDNY folder, which also holds `pension_manual_tier_3.pdf`, the Tier 3 Enhanced SPD revised Dec. 2019), the Fire Pension Fund's Annual Comprehensive Financial Report for FY2025 (in the owner's Google Drive as `fire-pension-fund-cafr.pdf`; summary of Tier I and II benefits and the notes), and page 27 of the Tiers 1 & 2 Summary Plan Description dated Sept. 12, 2014 (vesting only; PDF from the owner). Rules the page implements, all owner decisions (Oct. 11, 2026):

- **Service pension:** 50% of final salary at 20 years, plus 1/60th of total earnings after the 20th anniversary, plus a pension from ITHP contributions after 20 years, plus an annuity from excess contributions. No cap. Slider 20 to 40 years (stops 20, 25, 30, 35, 40; 25 and 35 on the lower row).
- **Wording:** the page always says "final average salary" (the SPD's glossary term), never "final salary", on both pages (owner, Oct. 12, 2026). The one exception is the Tier 3 fine print, which quotes S5058A's own words ("final salary").
- **Final salary (Oct. 12, 2026; from the Fund's Tiers 1 & 2 Summary Plan Description, Sept. 12, 2014, glossary "Final Average Salary" and Q&A, and Chapter 372 of the Laws of 2000):** the higher of (a) pensionable earnings in the 12 months before retirement, capped at 120% of the 12 months before that, or (b) the best 3-year period ending on the retirement month and day (or whole years earlier), each 12-month block capped at 120% of the average of the two blocks before it. Overtime included. The SPD gives (b) only to members appointed before July 1, 2000 (later appointees: last 12 months only); Chapter 540 of the Laws of 2021 (S6405 Gounardes / A7640 Abbate; effective Oct. 29, 2021, per the fiscal note of 2023 bill S5571) gave all Tier 2 members the same calculation, so the page offers (b) to everyone. Found by web search Oct. 12, 2026 from the bills' sponsor memos; the chapter text itself wasn't read (nysenate.gov is blocked from the session). S5571 (2023), which would make it retroactive to July 1, 2000 for those already retired, shows no sign of passing. (The owner first thought the last year was uncapped; the SPD says otherwise and he chose to follow it.) The personal calculator splits calendar years evenly by month to build 12-month blocks; the earnings table starts at the appointment year (owner, Oct. 12, 2026), marks the years used ("Best 3" or "Final 12"), and shows any amount over the 20% limit. The quick calculator uses the typed final salary as is and assumes each year past 20 paid about that much.
- **ITHP:** estimated as 5% of pay after the 20th anniversary (whole career on a 3/4s), credited 8.25% a year to retirement, then paid level to age 85 at 7%. The 7%/age-85 payout is our own assumption, labeled rough. The quick calculator figures it on the typed final salary, assuming appointment at age 25, and shows it past 20 years or on a 3/4s. It also mentions the banked variable lump sum.
- **Excess contributions (Oct. 12, 2026, after a Tier 2 member's feedback; owner's decisions):** two optional boxes, "Taxable excess" and "Non-taxable excess", as ESS shows them, in both calculators. They replace the old single "Your ITHP and annuity from the Fund" override box, which is gone. The personal numbers page grows the total at 8.25% a year from today to the retirement date (no new contributions); the quick calculator uses it as typed. Shown as a lump sum and as an estimated annuity (same rough payout as ITHP), added to the total as the annuity. Taxability doesn't change the amount, only taxes (the hint explains). The member asked for an exact monthly figure: that needs the Fund's annuity conversion tables, which we don't have. Ask the owner for one or more real Fund estimates showing an excess balance and the annuity it buys, then calibrate `annuityFactor`. On a 3/4s the whole contribution account becomes an annuity; the page counts only what's typed.
- **Longevity bonus (added Oct. 12, 2026, owner's decision):** same rule, rank picker, top-pay table and "not on 3/4s" as Tier 3, added to final salary at 25+ years. Basis: S9202 / A10392 (PDFs from the owner) add Admin Code § 15-110.1 for any member who "retires in any rank", adding the bonus to "the applicable salary used for … computing pension benefits under the plan in which they are enrolled"; the fiscal note (2026-12) gives a Tier 2 example (32-year FF, ~$10,143/yr with inflated top pay) and bases costs on Tier 2 experience. Confirmed Oct. 12, 2026: the enacted text (S9008-C/A10008-C Part AAA) is identical. Shown in Pension basics, the fine print, the comparison table and as a Bills law card.
- **COLA only, no escalation; no Social Security offset** (the ACFR lists the offset only for Tier 3). COLA same rule as Tier 3; it reduces the VSF until 62.
- **VSF $12,000 and banked variable** as in Tier 3 (ACFR notes: max $12,000 since 2008 for firefighters and officers).
- **3/4s:** 75% of final salary + 1/60th after 20 + ITHP reserve + annuity of accumulated deductions; the 75% is federally tax-free, the rest federally taxed, all free of NYS/NYC tax. No VSF. COLA after 5 years retired.
- **Comparison:** "Retire soon or work longer?": the soonest date ahead (20 years or next month), then +1, +3 and +5 years, plus "Your plan". No escalation chart.
- **Age limit:** the page warns past 65 and the goal search stops at 65 or 40 years. The Tier 2 mandatory retirement age has not been verified.
- **Longevity (both tiers, from both SPDs):** pension counts longevity only at the 10-year level from 20 to under 25 years, and at the 20-year level at 25+. Explained in Pension basics and the fine print on both pages (owner, Oct. 12, 2026); the math does not adjust yet (it uses full W-2 pay). To adjust, take longevity amounts from the UFA/UFOA schedules in the owner's Drive FDNY folder, after checking with him.
- Not modeled: ordinary disability, vested retirement, other credited service (1/80th), loans and shortages, Tier 1, options.
