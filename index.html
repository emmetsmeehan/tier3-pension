FAS calculator

What is my final average salary?

Enter your pensionable earnings for each calendar year. Your W-2 Box 5 (Medicare wages) is a good starting point, minus non-pensionable pay like uniform allowance. Future years are filled in at your pay growth assumption; type over any of them.

  • Best 3 in a rowYour FAS is the highest average of any 3 consecutive calendar years.
  • 10% anti-spiking capA year can count for at most 110% of the average of the 2 years before it. Anything above that is cut.
  • Full years onlyThis worksheet uses complete calendar years before the year you retire. The Fund may also use the 36 months right before your retirement date.

Fill in your real earnings from NYCAPS

Easiest on a computer. What you paste stays in your browser and is never sent anywhere.

  1. Sign in to NYCAPS Employee Self Service.
  2. Click the Pay and Tax Information tile.
  3. In the menu on the left, open Pay and Tax Information, then click Tax Summary (W-2, 1127 & 1095C).
  4. In the Tax Summary for All Years table, drag from the word Year down to the last row to highlight it, then copy it (Ctrl+C, or ⌘+C on a Mac).
  5. Paste it in the box below and click Fill in my earnings.

This uses the Medicare Wages column: all your taxable pay, overtime included, without the Social Security cap. It is close to what the Pension Fund counts but not exact, so check each year and type over any that look off.

Common questions

Who is this for?

FDNY uniformed members in Tier 3, which means anyone appointed on or after July 1, 2009. If you were appointed before that, you are generally in Tier 2, which has different rules.

Why doesn't my pension percentage go up after 20 years?

Tier 3 pays 50% of your final average salary at 20 years and stays at 50% however long you work. Extra years still help in three ways: a higher final average salary, a better escalation rate, and one banked VSF payment for each year past 20.

What is final average salary?

The average of your best 3 calendar years in a row, overtime included. A year only counts up to 110% of the average of the two years before it. The FAS calculator above works yours out from your yearly earnings.

Why does everyone talk about 25 years?

If you start collecting at 25 years, your whole pension rises with inflation every year, up to 3%. Start earlier and that rate is cut by 1/36 for each month you are short, so at 22 years or less there is none. Two bills in Albany would move full escalation to 23 years (S9204) or 20 years (S9203). Neither is law yet; see Bills in Albany.

What if I go out on a 3/4s disability?

An accident disability pension pays 75% of your final average salary at any length of service, and it is generally not taxed. It has no escalation, no VSF or banked variable, and no Social Security offset at 62. After 5 years retired you get the COLA on the first $18,000, at any age. It must be approved by the Pension Fund's Medical Board and Board of Trustees. Check the 3/4s (Disabled) box in the calculator to see your numbers.

What happens to my pension at 62?

It drops by half of your Social Security benefit. Enter your own estimate from ssa.gov to see the effect on your numbers.

Is the 25-year longevity bonus real?

Not yet. There are two versions in Albany, both still in committee. S9306/A10438 adds 5% of your rank's top pay at 25 years, 10% at 30 and 15% at 35. S9202/A10392 adds 5% at 25 plus 1% for each year after, up to 15% at 35. You can pick either one in the calculator, or uncheck the box to see your numbers without it.

What are the VSF and the banked variable?

The Variable Supplements Fund pays $12,000 a year to service retirees with 20 or more years. For each year you work past 20, one payment is banked and paid to you as a lump sum when you retire.

Is anything I type saved or sent anywhere?

No. The math runs in your browser, and this page does not store or send the numbers you enter. The only thing ever sent is a question or suggestion you choose to submit in the box below.

How it's calculated

Pension: 50% of final average salary at 20+ years of service, under S4727 (Chapter 692 of the Laws of 2025, signed Dec. 19, 2025). Tier 3 is capped at 50%, so the percentage does not grow past 20 years.

25-year longevity bonus: for members retiring with 25+ years, part of the top base pay for their rank is added to final average salary. Under S9306/A10438 (the default here) it is 5% at 25 years, 10% at 30 and 15% at 35, with the years in between at the lower step. Under S9202/A10392 it is 5% at 25 plus 1% for each year after, capped at 15% at 35. For Tier 3 it is added straight to FAS and is not limited by the 10% anti-spiking cap. Both are proposed bills, still in committee as of October 2026 and not law, so the terms may change. Rank pay is the top-step annual base salary from the UFA schedule effective Aug. 1, 2024 (firefighter) and the UFOA schedule effective July 31, 2025 (lieutenant, captain, battalion chief), with no longevity, night differential or overtime. The bill says “highest grade of pay” without defining it, so the figure used could end up higher than base salary.

FAS: average of the highest 3 consecutive years. Any year that exceeds the average of the prior two years by more than 10% counts only up to that 110% line. Unpaid leave (e.g. suspension) inside the FAS period is swapped for earlier time; the worksheet doesn't model that.

Escalation: the lesser of CPI or 3%, applied to the whole pension each April and compounding every year. Full escalation requires retiring at 25 years or more. The rate is reduced 1/36 for each month short of 25 years, so retiring at 22 years or earlier gets none. This calculator assumes payments start when you retire. Proposed bills would move full escalation to 23 years, with partial escalation after 20 (S9204/A11269), or to 20 years (S9203); you can try either in the calculator.

COLA: without full escalation, the pension gets the greater of escalation or the city COLA: half of CPI (1% to 3%) on the first $18,000 only, starting at age 62 after 5 years retired, or age 55 after 10 years retired. COLA reduces the VSF until 62.

VSF and banked variable: service retirees with 20+ years get the VSF, $12,000 a year. Each year worked past 20 banks one VSF payment, paid as a lump sum at retirement (rollover-eligible).

3/4s disability: an accident disability retirement pays 75% of final average salary, with no minimum service. It is generally not taxable. There is no escalation, no VSF or banked variable, no longevity bonus, and no Social Security offset. The COLA (half of CPI, 1% to 3%, on the first $18,000) starts after 5 years retired, regardless of age. Source: the Fire Pension Fund's Tier 3 Enhanced Summary Plan Description.

Not included: salary earned during extra years worked, retirement options that reduce the pension for a survivor, taxes (service pensions are federally taxable, exempt from NYS/NYC tax), ordinary (non-line-of-duty) disability, and Tier 2 rules.
