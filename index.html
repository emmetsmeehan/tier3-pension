Common questions

Who is this for?

FDNY uniformed members in Tier 3, which means anyone appointed on or after July 1, 2009. If you were appointed before that, you are generally in Tier 2, which has different rules.

Why doesn't my pension percentage go up after 20 years?

Tier 3 pays 50% of your final average salary at 20 years and stays at 50% however long you work. Extra years still help in three ways: a higher final average salary, a better escalation rate, and one banked VSF payment for each year past 20.

What is final average salary?

The average of your best 3 calendar years in a row, overtime included. A year only counts up to 110% of the average of the two years before it. The FAS calculator above works yours out from your yearly earnings.

Why does everyone talk about 25 years?

If you start collecting at 25 years, your whole pension rises with inflation every year, up to 3%. Start earlier and that rate is cut by 1/36 for each month you are short, so at 22 years or less there is none.

What happens to my pension at 62?

It drops by half of your Social Security benefit. Enter your own estimate from ssa.gov to see the effect on your numbers.

Is the 25-year longevity bonus real?

Not yet. It is a bill in Albany (A10438/S9306) that has not passed. Uncheck the box in the calculator to see your numbers without it.

What are the VSF and the banked variable?

The Variable Supplements Fund pays $12,000 a year to service retirees with 20 or more years. For each year you work past 20, one payment is banked and paid to you as a lump sum when you retire.

Is anything I type saved or sent anywhere?

No. The math runs in your browser, and this page does not store or send what you enter.

Is this official?

How it's calculated

Pension: 50% of final average salary at 20+ years of service, under S4727 (Chapter 692 of the Laws of 2025, signed Dec. 19, 2025). Tier 3 is capped at 50%, so the percentage does not grow past 20 years.

25-year longevity bonus: for members retiring with 25+ years, 5% of the top base pay for their rank is added to final average salary, rising to 10% at 30 years and 15% at 35. The years in between stay at the lower step. For Tier 3 it is added straight to FAS and is not limited by the 10% anti-spiking cap. This is a proposed bill (A10438/S9306). It was still in committee as of October 2026 and is not law, so the terms may change. Rank pay is the top-step annual base salary from the UFA schedule effective Aug. 1, 2024 (firefighter) and the UFOA schedule effective July 31, 2025 (lieutenant, captain, battalion chief), with no longevity, night differential or overtime. The bill says “highest grade of pay” without defining it, so the figure used could end up higher than base salary.

FAS: average of the highest 3 consecutive years. Any year that exceeds the average of the prior two years by more than 10% counts only up to that 110% line. Unpaid leave (e.g. suspension) inside the FAS period is swapped for earlier time; the worksheet doesn't model that.

Escalation: the lesser of CPI or 3%, applied to the whole pension each April and compounding every year. Full escalation requires retiring at 25 years or more. The rate is reduced 1/36 for each month short of 25 years, so retiring at 22 years or earlier gets none. This calculator assumes payments start when you retire.

COLA: without full escalation, the pension gets the greater of escalation or the city COLA: half of CPI (1% to 3%) on the first $18,000 only, starting at age 62 after 5 years retired, or age 55 after 10 years retired. COLA reduces the VSF until 62.

VSF and banked variable: service retirees with 20+ years get the VSF, $12,000 a year. Each year worked past 20 banks one VSF payment, paid as a lump sum at retirement (rollover-eligible).

Not included: salary earned during extra years worked, retirement options that reduce the pension for a survivor, taxes (service pensions are federally taxable, exempt from NYS/NYC tax), disability pensions, and Tier 2 rules.
